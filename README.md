One line, at most 200 characters: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup — Coo-Working, statue-head desk space for pigeons, zero churn, and a burn rate of one bagel. The pitch is written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`, the path the program/v1 harness reads.

The pitch (169 characters):

> Coo-Working sublets statue heads as desk space to 40,000 pigeons a day -- zero churn, and our burn rate is one bagel. Seed round's open -- bring crumbs, not term sheets.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This prints the pitch and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_PATH`). No network, no randomness: the same line on every run.
